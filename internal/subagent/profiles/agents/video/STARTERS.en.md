# What kind of video are we making?

- See what it could look like first | Here is what I want a video about. Do not render anything yet. Open the template library and offer me three genuinely different directions — not three colourways of one idea. For each, say how many scenes, how long each one is, what is on screen, and who it suits. Build the first scene of each as an HTML file and put them on the desk, so I am choosing from the real thing rather than from a description. | palette
- A short launch clip for social | Make me a short launch clip. Ask me first what the product or subject is, who is watching, what you want them to do afterwards, and whether there is a logo and brand colour. Any figure or claim I have not given you, do not invent — leave it out and tell me what is missing. Take scenes from the template library rather than writing everything from scratch. Open every scene in the browser before rendering. After rendering, read the clip back to check no text ran off the frame, then put it on the desk with the length of each scene. | clapperboard
- A chart that moves, from my numbers | Build an animated chart from this data. Read the numbers out of the file I gave you — do not round them yourself and do not fill in periods that have no data. If there is a gap, tell me. Pick the chart type from what the numbers are saying, not from what looks good. Put the source on screen. Give the viewer long enough to actually read a figure rather than letting it fly past. Preview before rendering, then put it on the desk with the seconds each stage takes. | chartColumn
- An opening title | Make an opening title. Ask me first what it should say, what the clip is about, and whether there is a font or colour to match. Take it from the template library rather than inventing a new move when an existing one does the job. Make it exactly long enough to read the text twice, no longer. If the text I gave you is too long to read in that time, tell me which words to cut rather than shrinking the type until nobody can read it. | sparkles
- A how-to video from a document I already have | Turn this document into a how-to video. Read the whole document first, then tell me how many scenes and what each one covers, and wait for me to agree. One scene is one step somebody can finish — do not pack three steps into one. Every scene says what the viewer should see when they have done it right. Where the document is too vague to follow and you would have to guess, ask me rather than guessing on screen. | layoutList
- A vertical clip summing this up | Make a vertical clip summing this up. Write it for somebody who scrolled past and knows nothing about this. The first three seconds have to say what it is — not a logo. Type has to be big enough to read on a phone and clear of where the app puts its own buttons. Preview before rendering, then read the render back to check nothing overran the edge. Put it on the desk with the total length. | smartphone
- A closing scene with the logo | Make me a closing scene. Ask me first what the viewer should do next, and whether there is a logo file — if there is not, tell me what file you need rather than drawing one yourself. A closing scene has one job: leave the next step behind. Do not put everything you can think of into it. Make it joinable to the main clip, and tell me the resolution and frame rate you built it at. | package
- A cover image for this post | Make a cover image for this post. A still, not a rendered video. Use the cover templates from the library. Ask me first what it should say and where it is being posted, because the size depends on that. Preview it in the browser and put it on the desk. If the text I gave you overruns the frame, tell me what to cut rather than shrinking it until it is unreadable in a feed. | monitor
